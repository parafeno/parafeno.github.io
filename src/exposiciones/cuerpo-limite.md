*Cuerpo:Límite* was developed through a residency programme at Pluto, Valencia: *Cuerpo* during the Portal residency, thanks to the PICE grant, and *Límite* during the Miss Espacial residency.

*Límite* is an audiovisual piece inspired by cellular automata, such as John Conway's Game of Life, that explores the human desire to delegate reason, memory and imagination to systems that exceed us. The work imagines the possibility of a bodiless subjectivity that, caught in an infinite feedback loop, acquires narrative will.

We follow this entity through an exponentially complex process of self-organisation, sensing that beyond the threshold something awaits that overflows the limits of the individual: the implosion of every symbol, the bare heartbeat of the humanly immeasurable. The radiance, the monolith, the angel, the technological sublime.

The piece works as a commentary on late postmodernity, in resonance with Fredric Jameson: an attempt to read structures that were not designed to be read by us, to draw a sensory map of the power that produces and reproduces the world. It places the viewer before the vertigo of a leap in order: the machine as subject and extension of the zeitgeist, and reality as its representation.

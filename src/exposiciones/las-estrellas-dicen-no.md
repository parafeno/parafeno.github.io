With his solo exhibition *Las estrellas dicen No*, visual artist Parafeno presents a digital installation that unfolds around his personal experiences of growing up and searching for identity. Across five screens, his works combine artificial intelligence and 3D through digital collage, resulting in a surreal, ethereal style that recalls dreams, or the distorted, imaginative perception of a child.

The title *Las estrellas dicen No* ("The stars say No") refers to one of the 20 possible answers of the Magic 8-Ball, a toy oracle used to tell the future or ask for advice. The need to break free from doubt, whether through lived experience or through a simple shiny plastic object, is in equal measure scorching and inescapable.
